Samsung Galaxy F70 5G Launched in India: Price, Specifications and Features

Samsung Galaxy F70 5G features a 120Hz sAMOLED display, Exynos 1330 chipset, 50MP OIS camera and 5,000mAh battery. Check its price and sale date in India.

By HammidaTech Editorial Team • 19 October 2026 • 3 min read

Samsung has launched the Galaxy F70 5G in India, expanding its F-series smartphone lineup. The new phone combines a high-refresh-rate sAMOLED display, an Exynos processor, optical image stabilisation for the main camera and a 5,000mAh battery. Here is a look at its price, specifications and availability.

Samsung Galaxy F70 5G Price in India

The Samsung Galaxy F70 5G starts at ₹19,999 for the 4GB RAM and 128GB storage variant. The 6GB RAM and 128GB storage model costs ₹22,999. The smartphone is offered in Bold Black and Vivid Violet colour options.

According to the reported launch details, the phone is scheduled to go on sale through Samsung India's website and Flipkart from October 20, 2026.

6.7-Inch sAMOLED Display With 120Hz Refresh Rate

The Galaxy F70 5G features a 6.7-inch flat sAMOLED display with Full HD+ resolution and a 120Hz refresh rate. The higher refresh rate can make scrolling and supported animations look smoother. A side-mounted fingerprint sensor is included for unlocking the device.

Exynos 1330 Processor and Storage

Samsung has equipped the smartphone with the Exynos 1330 chipset. It comes with up to 6GB of LPDDR4x RAM and 128GB of internal storage. A microSD card slot is also available for users who need additional storage space.

50MP Camera With Optical Image Stabilisation

The phone features a 50-megapixel primary rear camera with optical image stabilisation (OIS), which Samsung markets as its No Shake Cam. OIS helps reduce blur caused by small hand movements. For selfies and video calls, the device includes a 13-megapixel front camera.

5,000mAh Battery and 25W Charging

The Galaxy F70 5G packs a 5,000mAh battery and supports 25W wired charging. Buyers should note that the compatible 25W charging adapter is sold separately, according to the launch report.

Design and Durability

The smartphone measures 7.5mm in thickness and comes with Corning Gorilla Glass Victus display protection. It also carries an IP54 rating for dust and splash resistance. This rating does not mean the phone is waterproof, so users should avoid exposing it to excessive water.

Samsung Galaxy F70 5G Specifications

  • Display: 6.7-inch sAMOLED, Full HD+, 120Hz
  • Processor: Samsung Exynos 1330
  • RAM: 4GB / 6GB LPDDR4x
  • Storage: 128GB, expandable via microSD
  • Rear camera: 50MP with OIS
  • Front camera: 13MP
  • Battery: 5,000mAh
  • Charging: 25W wired
  • Connectivity: 5G
  • Protection: Gorilla Glass Victus, IP54 rating
  • Colours: Bold Black and Vivid Violet
  • Starting price: ₹19,999

Should You Consider the Samsung Galaxy F70 5G?

The Galaxy F70 5G offers a combination of a 120Hz sAMOLED display, a stabilised 50MP camera and a large battery. However, buyers should also compare its processor, charging speed and available RAM with competing phones in the same price range before making a decision.

Conclusion

With a starting price of ₹19,999, the Samsung Galaxy F70 5G targets buyers looking for a Samsung smartphone with a smooth display and optical image stabilisation. Its overall value will depend on real-world performance, camera results and competing offers when sales begin.
