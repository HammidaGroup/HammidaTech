Apple's First Touchscreen MacBook Pro Coming Soon? Big Reveal Expected on October 28!

Apple could unveil a touchscreen MacBook Pro at its October 28 event. Here's what reports suggest about its OLED display and expected features.

By HammidaTech Editorial Team • 20 Oct 2026 • 4 min read

Apple could be preparing a major change to its MacBook lineup. According to reports, the company may unveil a touchscreen MacBook Pro at its October 28, 2026 event. If introduced, the device could offer users a new way to interact with macOS.

What Could Apple Announce on October 28?

Apple has announced a product event titled "Pro and tell" for October 28. Reports suggest that new Mac devices could be among the highlights. However, Apple has not officially confirmed the launch of a touchscreen MacBook Pro.

MacBook Pro Could Finally Get a Touchscreen

According to reports based on information from Bloomberg, a future MacBook Pro could allow users to interact directly with the display. This could provide an additional input option alongside the traditional keyboard and trackpad.

OLED Display and Dynamic Island-Like Features

An OLED display is among the rumored upgrades. OLED technology can deliver deeper blacks and improved contrast. Reports have also discussed a Dynamic Island-style interface inspired by the iPhone. Final specifications remain unconfirmed.

Which Processors Could Power the New MacBook Pro?

Reports have suggested that a high-end touchscreen MacBook Pro could use Apple's M5 Pro and M5 Max chips. A separate 14-inch MacBook Pro featuring an M6 chip has also been discussed. These could be different models with different specifications.

Could the Price Be Higher?

OLED display technology and touchscreen hardware could increase the cost of the new MacBook Pro compared with existing models. However, Apple has not announced official pricing, availability in India, or a confirmed sales date.

What Else Could Apple Announce?

Reports have also discussed possible updates to other Apple devices, including the iMac and iPad mini. These expectations should be treated as rumors until Apple officially announces the products and their specifications.

Where Did the Touchscreen MacBook Pro Rumor Come From?

Discussions about Apple's touchscreen MacBook Pro did not start out of nowhere. They are driven by reports from the technology industry and expectations from analysts. Some reports have also mentioned the possibility of Apple unveiling such a MacBook at its October 28 event.

However, it is important to understand that an analyst's prediction or a media report is not an official announcement from the company. Until Apple confirms the new MacBook, details about its touchscreen, OLED display, and launch date should be treated as possibilities rather than confirmed facts.

Is This News True or Just a Rumor?

At present, it would not be accurate to describe the claims about the touchscreen MacBook Pro as confirmed news. A possibility mentioned in a report and a fact announced by Apple are two different things. Readers should check Apple's official website or event announcements before relying on any rumored feature or launch date.

Our goal is not just to share viral tech stories, but also to explain the information behind them. That's why we clearly distinguish between reports, predictions, and official confirmations, helping readers avoid mistaking rumors for facts.
