Microsoft Offers Buyouts to 60,000 Employees, India Included

Microsoft is offering voluntary exit buyouts to around 60,000 eligible employees across India, the US, UK and Canada. Here's who may qualify, which teams are included and why the company is making the move.

By HammidaTech Editorial Team • 27 October 2026 • 7 min read

Microsoft is offering voluntary buyouts to around 60,000 employees, according to reports published on October 26, 2026. The programme covers eligible employees in selected teams across the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom and India. The move comes as the technology company continues to invest heavily in artificial intelligence (AI) and looks for ways to manage its workforce and costs.

The announcement has drawn attention because Microsoft had approximately 223,000 full-time employees globally as of June 2026. The reported eligibility figure represents more than a quarter of that workforce, although it does not mean that all eligible employees will leave the company.

Importantly, this is a voluntary exit offer rather than an announcement that 60,000 people will automatically lose their jobs. Eligible employees can review the terms and decide whether the offer suits their circumstances.

What Is Microsoft's 60,000 Employee Exit Offer?

Microsoft's Voluntary Exit Offer gives eligible employees an option to leave the company with financial support and career transition resources. According to the company's announcement, the programme is time-limited and is intended to give employees considering opportunities outside Microsoft another choice.

The company has also said the programme can help participating teams adjust to changing business needs. Eligibility depends on the participating organisation, the employee's internal level and the programme's specific conditions. It is not available uniformly across every Microsoft department.

Employees who qualify are expected to receive details of their individual offers directly from the company. The terms may vary depending on factors such as role, level and length of service.

Microsoft Employees in India Are Also Included

India is among the four countries included in the voluntary exit programme, alongside the United States, the United Kingdom and Canada. Business Standard reported that Microsoft has around 23,000 employees in India, although the company has not publicly clarified how many Indian employees will qualify.

The offer is available to eligible employees at internal level 70 and below in participating teams. The exact teams and individual eligibility conditions matter, so employees should not assume that every person working for Microsoft in India can participate.

According to Business Standard, eligible Indian employees have until December 1 to decide on the offer. Employees should confirm their own deadline and package details through official internal communications rather than relying only on general news reports.

Which Microsoft Departments Are Included?

Reports citing people familiar with the programme say participating teams include AI experiences, Copilot, finance, human resources, Xbox, and Windows + Devices. These areas span Microsoft's consumer products, software, gaming and internal operations.

However, some teams are excluded. Reports say the programme does not cover certain employees in marketing, commercial business and security, while other exclusions may depend on business priorities. Microsoft has emphasised that participation varies according to customer commitments, workforce requirements and the needs of individual organisations.

For that reason, the reported figure of around 60,000 refers to potential eligibility, not a confirmed number of departures. Microsoft has not announced how many employees are expected to accept the offer.

Why Is Microsoft Offering Buyouts Now?

Microsoft is investing substantial sums in AI infrastructure, cloud computing and data centres. These investments require expensive equipment, computing capacity and energy, increasing the importance of managing operating costs while continuing to develop new products.

The company has integrated AI features into products such as Windows and Microsoft 365 through Copilot. It is also competing with major technology companies to expand its cloud and AI services. The voluntary exit programme comes as Microsoft seeks to adapt its workforce to changing priorities and spending requirements.

However, the company has not said that artificial intelligence alone is responsible for the buyout offer. The programme is presented as a way to respond to changing business needs, and its exact financial impact will depend on how many eligible employees accept.

Does the Offer Mean Microsoft Is Laying Off 60,000 People?

No. There is an important difference between a voluntary buyout and a compulsory layoff. Under a voluntary programme, eligible employees can choose whether to accept the offer, subject to its terms. The company has not confirmed that 60,000 employees will leave.

The programme also follows other workforce changes at Microsoft, including earlier voluntary retirement offers and job cuts reported during 2026. These developments show that the company is continuing to review its workforce, but the latest buyout offer should not be described as 60,000 confirmed job losses.

HammidaTech's Take

Microsoft's voluntary exit offer is significant because it potentially reaches a large number of employees, including eligible workers in India. It also highlights the challenge facing major technology companies as they balance investment in AI with workforce costs and changing business priorities.

For employees, the key question is whether the individual offer provides enough financial support to make leaving worthwhile. For the wider technology industry, the important measure will be how many employees accept and how Microsoft adjusts its teams afterward.

For now, the headline figure is around 60,000 eligible employees—not 60,000 confirmed departures. The final outcome will depend on employee decisions and the programme's terms.
