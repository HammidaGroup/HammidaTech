iQOO Pad Pro Announced: 165Hz OLED Display, 9,020mAh Battery & Gaming Features

iQOO has announced its compact gaming tablet with an 8.8-inch 165Hz OLED display, Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 processor, built-in cooling fan, and a 9,020mAh battery.

By HammidaTech Editorial Team • 20 Oct 2026 • 4 min read

iQOO has officially announced its new compact gaming tablet, the iQOO Pad Pro. Designed for users who want powerful gaming hardware in a portable form factor, the tablet features a high-refresh-rate OLED display, a large battery, and an integrated cooling fan.

According to Notebookcheck, the tablet will go on sale in China on October 28, 2026. However, the company has not yet announced its retail price or confirmed availability outside China.

iQOO Pad Pro Specifications

  • Display: 8.8-inch OLED panel
  • Refresh rate: 165Hz
  • Processor: Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5
  • Battery: 9,020mAh
  • Cooling: Built-in cooling fan
  • Gaming features: Touch and gyroscope controls
  • Sale date: October 28, 2026, in China
  • Price: Not announced yet

8.8-Inch OLED Display With a 165Hz Refresh Rate

One of the tablet's main highlights is its 8.8-inch OLED display with a 165Hz refresh rate. The compact screen size makes the device easier to hold during gaming sessions, while the high refresh rate can provide smoother motion in supported games.

Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 Processor

The iQOO Pad Pro is powered by Qualcomm's Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 processor. It also includes a dedicated Q3 gaming chip designed to support gaming-related processing. Actual performance will depend on the game, software, and thermal conditions.

Built-In Cooling Fan for Gaming

Another notable feature is the integrated cooling fan. Active cooling can help manage heat during demanding tasks and extended gaming sessions. However, real-world results will depend on the tablet's cooling system and workload.

9,020mAh Battery for Longer Sessions

The tablet packs a 9,020mAh battery. This large capacity should provide useful power reserves for gaming and entertainment, although actual battery life will vary depending on brightness, refresh rate, network use, and the games being played.

When Will the iQOO Pad Pro Go on Sale?

iQOO has confirmed October 28, 2026, as the sale date in China. The company has not yet disclosed the retail price, memory configurations, or availability in other countries, including India.

Is the iQOO Pad Pro Launch Confirmed?

Yes. The iQOO Pad Pro has been officially announced, and key specifications including its display, processor, and battery have been disclosed. The price and international launch details remain unconfirmed. Readers should wait for an official announcement before relying on any rumored pricing or India launch date.

HammidaTech's Take

The iQOO Pad Pro stands out for combining a compact 8.8-inch screen with a 165Hz OLED panel, a flagship-class processor, and active cooling. These features make it worth watching for mobile gaming enthusiasts. Its final value will depend on pricing, sustained performance, and whether iQOO brings it to markets beyond China.
