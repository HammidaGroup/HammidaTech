Useful AI tools for studying, research, writing and revision—plus tips to use them responsibly.

By HammidaTech Editorial Team • 18 October 2026 • 5 min read

AI tools can help students understand difficult topics, organise notes, practise questions and improve their writing. They are useful study assistants, but they can make mistakes, so always verify important information.

1. ChatGPT — explanations and practice

Ask ChatGPT to explain a topic in simple language, create practice questions or help you plan a revision schedule. Try answering questions yourself before checking the explanation.

2. Google Gemini — ideas and learning support

Gemini can help brainstorm project ideas and explain concepts. For assignments, check the original sources and confirm that the information is accurate.

3. Perplexity — research starting points

Research tools can help you discover sources on a topic. Open the cited sources yourself and make sure they actually support the claims you use.

4. Grammarly — writing feedback

Writing assistants can identify grammar and clarity issues. Use suggestions to improve your own work and follow your school's academic-integrity rules.

5. Canva — presentations and visual projects

Canva can help create slides, posters and visual study material. Keep designs readable and verify any facts included in them.

Tips for using AI safely

  • Check important answers using trusted sources.
  • Do not share passwords or private personal information.
  • Check current free-plan limits on each tool's official website.
  • Use AI to learn, not to submit work dishonestly.

Conclusion

Start with one tool that matches your study needs. Use it to practise and understand concepts, while relying on trusted sources for verification. Features and free-plan limits can change over time.
